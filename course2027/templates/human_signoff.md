# Human sign-off

Attach to **every** deliverable. Three lines, specific to this week's work. "I checked everything" is not specific. "I traced the Fairfield County value back to the source file" is.

```
What I checked myself:
What I'm trusting the AI on:
What I won't sign:
```

- **What I checked myself**: the checks you ran with your own eyes (a row count, a trace, a definition you looked up).
- **What I'm trusting the AI on**: the parts you didn't verify, for example that a chart's code draws what it says. Being honest here is a strength, not a weakness.
- **What I won't sign**: a number, claim or use you're not willing to stand behind yet, and why. "Nothing" is allowed only if you mean it.
